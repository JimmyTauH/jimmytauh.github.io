I am **Zhentao He** (James He), a Ph.D. student at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/) in the [ISTBI](https://istbi.fudan.edu.cn/) (Institute of Science and Technology for Brain-inspired Intelligence), working under the supervision of [Prof. Shanfeng Zhu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Aq6tXksAAAAJ). I am also a **Zhuobo Fellow at Fudan University** (university doctoral excellence program). My research focuses on **machine learning and data mining for biological data**. I am interested in developing robust, interpretable, and transferable representations, with applications to multiomics analysis, perturbation modeling, and protein function.

I received my B.E. in Computer Science & Technology from [Wuhan University](https://en.whu.edu.cn/) (2021–2025), where I was a **Hongyi Honor College student** and graduated as an **Outstanding Graduate of Wuhan University (Class of 2025)**. My undergraduate research at the [BIOD Lab](http://biod.whu.edu.cn/en/index.htm) focused on single-cell multiomics data integration methods under the guidance of [Prof. Lihua Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=sUmlrP8AAAAJ).

**Research Interests:**
- Representation Learning
- Generalization & Adaptation
- Multimodal Learning for Multiomics
- Perturbation Prediction & Protein Function Modeling

I am currently seeking opportunities for research visits and joint doctoral training, and I am always happy to discuss potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:zthe25@m.fudan.edu.cn) or check out my [GitHub](https://github.com/JimmyTauH)!
